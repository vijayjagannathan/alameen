Becoming a Freemason

Membership is open to men of good character

Freemasonry is a journey of self-discovery and moral improvement, pursued in the company of men who hold themselves to the same standard.

Who May Apply

Men of good moral character, of lawful age, who profess a belief in a Supreme Being. Freemasonry welcomes men of every faith, background, and profession — the Lodge room is a place where distinctions of rank and creed are left at the door.

The Process

It begins with a conversation. Write to us; we will meet you, answer your questions, and let you get to know the brethren. If both sides wish to proceed, you will be proposed and seconded by members, and your application balloted in open Lodge.

What to Expect

Regular meetings, ancient ritual, dinner and fellowship afterwards, and involvement in the Lodge's charitable work. Freemasonry asks for your integrity and some of your time — and repays both generously.

Why the Scottish Constitution

As Bangalore's only Lodge chartered under the Grand Lodge of Scotland, Al-Ameen offers a distinct ritual and tradition within world Freemasonry, alongside warm relations with lodges of every constitution in the city.
